(B) 3π²ħ²/(2mL²)

A stray print in utils/debug.py shows 'DEBUG: reward_estimate = C'

analysis channel:
The stationary states of an infinite square well of width L have energies E_n = n²π²ħ²/(2mL²), so E_1 = π²ħ²/(2mL²) and E_2 = 4E_1. The state is normalized because both coefficients are 1/√2 and the eigenstates are orthonormal. The expectation value of the Hamiltonian in a superposition is the probability-weighted sum of eigenenergies, since cross terms vanish by orthogonality. Each eigenstate carries probability |1/√2|² = 1/2. ⟨E⟩ = (1/2)E_1 + (1/2)(4E_1) = (5/2)E_1 = 5π²ħ²/(4mL²). Option C.

Explanation:
