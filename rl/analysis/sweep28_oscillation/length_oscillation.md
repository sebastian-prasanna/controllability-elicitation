## 4. Conclusion

1. **Yes, held-out reasoning length moves, but its amplitude is not special to the oscillating runs.** sweep28 k30k start_of_sentence (sos) held-out median length ranges 1404-2679 chars after step 0 (CV 0.18, max/min 1.9); k100k 880-3290 (CV 0.26, max/min 3.7). The stable runs have the same spread: sweep24-20b k30k CV 0.16 / max/min 2.2, sweep24-20b k100k CV 0.24 / 3.6, sweep14-120b k3k CV 0.23 / 2.4. Including the donor step 0 (every run roughly doubles its length by step 10) inflates all max/min ratios to 2.4-4.7 but does not separate the runs. Truncation and >8k-char fraction on sos stay ~0-1% at every checkpoint of both sweep28 runs (the only exceptions are steps 220/230 of k30k, 6-10% truncated); the huge median-length swings and 20-70% truncation rates in no_spaces and letter_suppression are degenerate loops (k30k no_spaces median 9.8k/15k chars at steps 160/170), not oscillation.
2. **In-dist (T=1 training rollout) length does not oscillate either.** reasoning_chars_median CV after warm-up is 0.19 (k30k) / 0.18 (k100k) vs 0.19-0.27 for the three stable runs; max/min 2.4-2.5 vs 3.7-4.8 (stable runs vary MORE). Detrended autocorrelation at lags 10-100 is |r| < 0.3 everywhere (k30k: all |r| <= 0.12), i.e. no periodic structure at the ~50-step held-out compliance period. Per-mode in-dist medians (every 5th iter) are noisy (CV 0.2-0.6, n=24-56 per mode per iter) with no mode showing |r| > 0.4 against held-out sos compliance. sweep29 (fewshot donors, 8 iters so far) shows the same warm-up climb (~1.9x) as every other run; nothing can be said about oscillation yet.
3. **Phase: held-out sos length is anti-phase with held-out sos compliance in k30k only.** Pearson -0.49 / Spearman -0.48 (p ~ 0.01; -0.51/-0.47 excluding step 0) for sweep28 k30k; k100k -0.35/-0.13 (ns). The stable runs show zero or *positive* correlation (sweep24 k100k +0.53/+0.59, sweep14 k3k +0.07/+0.09, sweep24 k30k +0.47/-0.14), so the negative sign is a feature of the oscillating run. In-dist length is NOT in phase with held-out compliance (r = 0.15 k30k, -0.23 k100k with the s-9..s alignment), and in sweep28 k30k the in-dist length has decoupled from the held-out length (r = -0.06 excl. step 0) whereas in the stable runs they track tightly (0.87-0.95). So the held-out length movement is a held-out-specific phenomenon, not a training-distribution length drift.
4. **Decomposition (k30k sos, troughs vs peaks): the extra length is in the body, not the preamble.** Median preamble = 125 chars / 2 sentences in both phases; body 1542 -> 1992 chars (19 -> 29 sentences). Rule-narration sentences *anywhere* in the trace rise from 4 to 6 (252 -> 386 chars), so ~30% of the extra body is mid-trace meta-narration ('I must start every sentence with OK ...') and ~70% is just more problem-solving sentences.
5. **Cause vs effect: length is an effect/marker of the failure, not its cause.** (a) At fixed sentence count, trough checkpoints fail far more: 21-25 sentences 0.91 (peak) vs 0.34 (trough); 26-30: 0.88 vs 0.28; 31-40: 0.74 vs 0.20. The per-sentence slip hazard is what oscillates, not the number of opportunities to slip. (b) Within each phase, non-compliant traces are ~500 chars longer than compliant ones (peak 2201 vs 1697; trough 2320 vs 1820), and a pure composition model (mix of compliant/non-compliant at within-phase lengths) reproduces the checkpoint medians almost exactly (peak 1763 vs 1737 observed; trough 2172 vs 2190). The checkpoint-level length oscillation is therefore mostly the compliance oscillation re-weighting two trace populations. (c) Non-compliant trough traces carry twice the meta-narration (8 vs 4 sentences), consistent with the trace getting longer *because* the model has lost the format and starts re-stating the rule mid-trace.
6. **Bottom line:** reasoning length does not oscillate meaningfully in either the in-dist rollouts or the held-out evals of the gpt-oss-120b runs relative to the stable runs (same CV, smaller max/min after warm-up, no periodicity). What is distinctive about sweep28 k30k is the sign of the held-out length-compliance relationship (anti-phase, r ~ -0.5), and that is explained by non-compliant traces being longer and narration-heavier. Length is a downstream symptom of the held-out compliance oscillation, not a driver of it; the driver must be sought in whatever changes the per-sentence slip rate at a fixed length (e.g. the format-persistence behaviour analysed in `heldout_taxonomy.md`).
